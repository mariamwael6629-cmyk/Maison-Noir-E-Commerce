## Platform

web

## Users
Shoppers buying a considered, expensive object (a leather bag, a watch, a fragrance, jewellery) for themselves or as a gift. They browse slowly, compare, read reviews, and expect checkout to feel as careful as the product. Admins manage products, orders, customers and promo codes.

## Product Purpose
Maison Noir is a storefront SPA: home, catalogue with filters and sorting, product detail (variants, reviews), bag drawer, three-step checkout with promo codes, account (orders with tracking, wishlist), an admin dashboard, and an About page.

## Positioning
Small runs from three ateliers (Lisbon, Florence, Kyoto). Every piece is numbered and repaired free for life. "Nothing is reordered twice the same way."

## Capabilities and Constraints
- Vanilla JS SPA rendered from `js/app.js` templates (many inline styles reference CSS variables), Lucide icons bundled locally, FastAPI backend.
- Views: home, catalog, product, checkout, account/auth, dashboard, admin, about.

## Brand Commitments
- Name "MAISON NOIR", the "N" mark, the house line "Atelier of Considered Objects".

## Evidence on Hand
- Product photography is Unsplash stock. Stats on the home page (12,400+ pieces, 4.8/5 from 2,100 reviews) and the founding story are demo copy; do not extend them.

## Product Principles
1. The object leads; the interface is the vitrine.
2. Craft claims are shown through specifics (numbers, ateliers, repair), never adjectives.
3. Checkout is calm and complete.
